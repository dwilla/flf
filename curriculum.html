Curriculum

Learn by playing: run a farm, then hatch a flock.

Preview: Farm Game Preview: Poultry Game

Fourth Light Farm

Play Full Screen

Pick a seed, click a soil plot to plant it, click again to water it, then harvest and sell your crops. Open the Journal in the game for a page on each plant. Works with a mouse, or by tapping on a tablet or phone. On a phone, turn it sideways.

Poultry Game

Play Full Screen

Hatch six heritage eggs. Keep the incubator warm and humid, turn the eggs, and candle them to find the ones that will not hatch. Then raise your chicks in a brooder. Press Help in the game for the rules. Works with a mouse, or by tapping on a tablet or phone. On a phone, turn it sideways.
