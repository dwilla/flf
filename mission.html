Our Mission

Growing the next generation of sustainable farmers.

We are dedicated to growing education, kindness, opportunity, and health within our communities by harnessing the transformative power of agriculture. Through meaningful connections, hands-on learning, and sustainable practices, we cultivate spaces where people and nature thrive together.

Our Scholarship Commitment

4 Scholarships — $500 Each

Every year from 2026 through 2028, our goal is to award 4 scholarships of $500 each to students specializing in agriculture, helping to remove financial barriers for the next generation of farmers, soil scientists, and sustainable agriculture leaders. See our full 3-year goals for more.
