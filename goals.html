Our Goals

Where we're headed, 2026 through 2028.

Here's what we're working toward over the next three years — building the people, places, and partnerships that carry out our mission of regenerative agriculture education.

Every Year, 2026–2028

4 Scholarships — $500 Each

Each year of this three-year plan, we're committed to awarding 4 scholarships of $500 each to students specializing in agriculture.

Apply Here!

2026

  • Establish a network of local farmers and educators to provide workshops.
  • Create curriculum focused on our mission of regenerative agriculture.
  • Work to expand into an ELO with the State of New Hampshire to offer an accredited elective program.

2027

  • Acquire space to provide education and teaching workshops, with our mission focused first.
  • Find a location to set up as a brick-and-mortar site, providing space for our team and students.
  • Work with the local area to discover what we can do to help with food security and donations for families.

2028

  • Have our ELO framework implemented with the NH Department of Education, along with an online resource portal.
  • Host workshops and educational events monthly for our community and others to support regenerative agriculture.
  • Host trades classes and provide a space for people to learn how to safely operate agriculture equipment.
