Text Messaging Terms & Conditions

Fourth Light Farm Text Alerts

Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Program description

Fourth Light Farm (“FLF,” “we,” “us”), a New Hampshire nonprofit, offers a text message program for our board members, volunteers and others who choose to receive our texts (the “Program”). Texts may include board meeting notices and reminders, volunteer scheduling and event reminders, announcements about our farm, programs and events, and thank-you messages.

2. How you opt in

You join the Program only by giving us your mobile number and checking a box that says you agree to receive text messages from Fourth Light Farm, either in the FLF Board Room app or on our volunteer sign-up page. You may also give consent in writing or in person to an FLF officer. Consent to receive texts is not required to volunteer, serve on the board, donate, or purchase anything. After you join, we may send a confirmation text.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies. You can expect up to 4 messages per week.

4. Costs

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan. FLF does not charge for the Program.

5. How to opt out

You can cancel at any time. Reply STOP to any text and you will be unsubscribed and receive no further texts from us. You may also email fourthlightfarm@gmail.com and ask us to remove your number.

6. Help

Reply HELP to any text for assistance, or contact us at fourthlightfarm@gmail.com or (603) 265-1380.

7. Carriers

Carriers and wireless providers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is subject to your carrier’s coverage and network availability.

8. Privacy

We respect your privacy. We use your mobile number only to send you FLF communications. We do not sell, rent or share mobile numbers, or text-message consent, with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We may disclose information only when required by prevailing law or legal process, to protect safety, or to our insurers, auditors and attorneys as needed. See our Volunteer Privacy Policy for details.

9. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to join the Program, or have a parent or legal guardian agree for you. You confirm that you are the account holder or have the account holder’s permission to use the mobile number you provide.

10. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The date at the top shows when they were last changed. If you keep receiving texts after a change, you accept the updated terms.
